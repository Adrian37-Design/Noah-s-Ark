Noah's Ark was established as an Anglican parish in Fairview, bringing together a small but passionate community of faith.

Growth

Construction of the permanent church building commenced, providing a dedicated space for worship and community gathering.

Ministry

Launch of dedicated youth ministry, women's fellowship, and community outreach programmes serving communities in and around the Greater Harare area (Haydon, Goodhope, Sandton, Fairview, Marlborough, and Westgate).

Over 100 members, one Sunday service, and 4 active ministries — Noah's Ark continues to grow in faith and service.
