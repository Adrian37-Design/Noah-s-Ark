"We are not just erecting stone and mortar, but raising a spiritual home for generations to worship, grow, and serve the communities in Fairview, Haydon, Goodhope, Sandton, Marlborough, and Westgate."

The St. Noah's Ark building project represents our commitment to establishing a permanent sanctuary. Guided by our faith and the generosity of our congregation, these 3D designs depict a modern church facility optimized for robust worship services, youth education, and community fellowship.

The Project

Key Project Features

01

Modern Sanctuary

Designed with seating for 300+ congregants, featuring a tall arching spire, stained glass windows, and premium acoustics.

02

Secure Parking Facility

Over 30 secure, paved parking spaces with integrated access roads to allow seamless traffic flow on Sundays.

03

Rector's Residence

An on-premises rectory/clergy house built alongside the sanctuary to support our resident pastoral team.

04

Lawn & Fellowship Garden

A beautifully landscaped garden and courtyard situated behind the church, perfect for outdoor fellowship events.

05

Educational Classrooms

Dedicated youth and Sunday School spaces to nurture our younger generation in a safe environment.
