Our Connection

The Anglican Diocese of Harare

Noah's Ark Anglican Church is a parish of the Anglican Diocese of Harare, part of the Church of the Province of Central Africa (CPCA). The Diocese encompasses Anglican parishes across Harare and surrounds, united in worship, mission, and fellowship.

As an Anglican parish, we hold to the historic Christian faith as expressed in the Apostles' Creed and the Nicene Creed, rooted in scripture, shaped by tradition, and responsive to the Holy Spirit.

The Province of Central Africa

The CPCA is a member province of the worldwide Anglican Communion, linking millions of Christians across Zimbabwe, Zambia, Botswana, and Malawi in a common faith and mission.

What Makes Us Anglican?

Scripture

The Holy Bible is the inspired Word of God and the supreme authority for all matters of faith and conduct.

Tradition

We are heirs to 2,000 years of Christian history, shaped by the creedal faith of the undivided Church.

Reason

Anglicanism holds scripture, tradition, and reason together — thinking carefully about faith and life in the world.
