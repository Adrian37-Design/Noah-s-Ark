WEBSITE NAVIGATION GUIDE - VIDEO SCRIPT
Noah's Ark Anglican Church, Fairview

Estimated duration: 2-3 minutes
Format: Offline MP4 video

==================================================

[0:00] INTRODUCTION
Hello, and welcome to Noah's Ark Anglican Church, Fairview. If you are new to our website, this short video will show you how to find everything you need — whether you are on a phone, tablet, or computer.

[0:15] HOMEPAGE OVERVIEW
This is our homepage. At the top, you will see our church logo and a simple menu. The large welcome banner tells you who we are. As you scroll down, you will see our Sunday service time, welcome message from the church, and a photo gallery showing life at Noah's Ark. You will also see our Jersey Day fundraising banner, which celebrates our recent church fair.

[0:40] THE MENU
Look at the top of the page. The menu has links to About, Worship, Events, Diocese, and Find Us. On a mobile phone, tap the menu icon to open the same links.

[0:55] ABOUT PAGE
Click on About to learn our story — our mission, values, and the leadership of the church. If you want to know who we are, start here.

[1:05] WORSHIP PAGE
Click on Worship to see our Sunday service times and the list of today's officials, such as the priest, reader, and intercessor. This page is updated regularly, so it always shows the current week's service information.

[1:20] EVENTS PAGE
Click on Events to see upcoming notices, church activities, and special services. If you want to know what is happening soon, this is the page to visit.

[1:30] DIOCESE PAGE
Click on Diocese to learn about our connection to the Anglican Diocese of Harare and our bishop.

[1:38] FIND US PAGE
Click on Find Us to get directions to the church. The page can automatically use your location to show you the best route from wherever you are.

[1:50] ADMIN / LOGIN NOTE
If you are an admin, you can scroll to the bottom of any page and click the scripture quote in the footer to access the admin login page.

[2:00] CLOSING
That is everything you need to navigate our website. We would love to meet you in person. Join us every Sunday from 9:00 AM to 11:30 AM. God bless you, and see you soon.
