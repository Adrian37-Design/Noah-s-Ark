What We Stand For

Pillars of Our Faith

Three commitments that shape everything we do at Noah's Ark.

Worship

We gather every Sunday to encounter the living God through prayer, scripture, song, and the Holy Eucharist.

Community

From youth groups to senior circles, there is a place for every soul in our growing parish family.

Outreach

Worship to serve communities in and around the Greater Harare area (Haydon, Goodhope, Sandton, Fairview, Marlborough, and Westgate) through community support, compassion, and action.
